  <h1>About</h1>

Hey, I'm **Aarsh** — a developer who writes about the things I find interesting: software, systems, design, and the occasional philosophical rabbit hole.

This blog is where I think in public. Some posts are tutorials, some are half-baked ideas, and some are just notes I didn't want to lose.

## What I work on

I spend most of my time building software, thinking about how systems fit together, and occasionally breaking things to see how they work.

## This blog

Built with [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/) and a handcrafted dark theme. No trackers, no ads, no JavaScript frameworks — just words and code.

The source is as simple as possible so I can focus on writing.
